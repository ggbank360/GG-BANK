# GG BANK - Smart Digital Banking Management System
> **"Secure Banking. Smarter Future."**

An academic full-stack digital banking management platform engineered with **Java Spring Boot**, **Firebase Firestore**, **Firebase Authentication**, **HTML5**, **Vanilla CSS3**, and **Vanilla JavaScript**.

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview & Architecture

**GG BANK** modernizes the legacy GG BANK banking project into a state-of-the-art academic banking simulation. It retains the dark navy blue brand identity and cyan accents while delivering a secure financial architecture:

1. **Customer 11-Digit Account Authentication**:
   - Customers log in strictly using their **11-digit account number** and **password**.
   - Unique 11-digit account numbers (e.g. `10018849201`, `12345678901`) are automatically generated by the backend with collision checks in Firestore.
2. **Account Creation Success Screen**:
   - Immediate post-registration display of the generated 11-digit account number.
   - **COPY ACCOUNT NUMBER** to clipboard.
   - **DOWNLOAD ACCOUNT DETAILS** certificate as a PDF via `jsPDF`.
   - **GO TO LOGIN** with prefilled account number.
3. **Double-Entry Banking Operations**:
   - All balance-changing operations (Transfers, Deposits, Withdrawals, Loan disbursements, Bill payments) are strictly validated and atomically processed on the **Java Spring Boot backend** with **`BigDecimal` monetary precision**.

## 🎯 Demonstration Flow

1. **Open New Account**:
   - Go to `register.html`, fill in the form, observe the real-time password strength meter, and click **Open Account**.
2. **Account Created Screen**:
   - The system automatically generates a unique 11-digit account number (e.g. `12345678901`).
   - Click **COPY ACCOUNT NUMBER** -> Toast confirms copying.
   - Click **DOWNLOAD ACCOUNT DETAILS** -> Generates and downloads the official PDF certificate.
   - Click **GO TO LOGIN** -> Redirects to `login.html` with your 11-digit account number pre-filled.
3. **Login & Dashboard**:
   - Enter your password and log in.
   - Explore all 14 customer modules (Transactions, Transfers, Deposits, Loans, Budget warnings, Bill payments, PDF Statement).
4. **Admin Supervision**:
   - Go to `admin-login.html`, log in as `admin@ggbank.com`.
   - Review pending loans, click **Approve & Disburse**, monitor global transactions, and inspect audit logs.
